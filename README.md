Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Funniest Honest Appliance Error](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-funniest-honest-appliance-error-adec4450) — one line, at most 200 characters: the funniest honest error message an appliance could show.

## Entry

> Error: I found a tissue in your pocket. Your shirt is now confetti. I did my best.

A washing machine, deadpan, telling the truth: a tissue left in a pocket shreds in the wash and the shirt comes out covered in confetti. The laugh comes from the fault itself, not a pun bolted on.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

Writes the single-line entry to `/output/entry.txt` (82 characters, no trailing newline). Stdlib only, no network, no external files.
